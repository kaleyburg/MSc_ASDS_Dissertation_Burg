\begin{spacing}{1.5}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Uses Optical Character Recognition to scrape text from over 18,000 UK general election (2010–2019) leaflets.
    \item Focuses on instances of environmental and climate mentions in local election campaigns.
    \item Investigates variance between constituencies, parties, and election years.
    \item Creates a novel dataset containing word counts of political topic mentions.
    \item Results highlight that campaign variance is primarily driven by weather differences, student population, and employment composition.
\end{itemize}
